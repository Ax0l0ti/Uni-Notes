



## Dataset Selected

Kaggle dataset [Air Pollution and Mental Health](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/thedevastator/air-pollution-and-mental-health) lead to [CitieS-Health Barcelona| Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/6503022#.Y8OrG9JBwUE) for the OG data location. 95 columns

**What it is:** 40 participants walking with an environmental sensor kit and an Empatica E4 wristband, and rating their wellbeing on a phone. All the streams were resampled to 1 Hz and normalised to [0, 1]. The environment readings and the labels were upsampled by linear interpolation.
