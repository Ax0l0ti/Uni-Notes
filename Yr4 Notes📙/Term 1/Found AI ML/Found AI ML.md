### Coursework 30% vs 70% Examed
- CW1 - Foundations of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Coursework (30%)
	**Details** - The coursework will consist of developing computer programs to solve practical problems in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning . Study time: 12 hours.
- Exam - Foundations of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Exam (70%)
	**Details** - Answerbook Pink (12 page) Students may use a calculator. Study time: 30 hours.

### Course Description

> [!info]+  Course Description
> Course Description copied from [Module Catalogue](https://courses.warwick.ac.uk/modules/2026/CS920-10)
> 
|                        | Content |
| ---------------------- | ------- |
| **Learning Outcomes:** | • A systematic understanding of the main algorithms used in modern AI research and practice.<br>• The capacity to apply and execute the main Machine Learning techniques for supervised and unsupervised learning, comparing and optimising their use against several applications.<br>• Design tailor-made learning algorithm for a specific problem.<br>• An appreciation of responsible and explainable AI, ethical considerations, and evaluating limitations of the technology. |
| **Synopsis:**          | Introduction to the main toolbox of modern AI and ML, touching upon the basics of supervised and unsupervised learning, delving deeper into Reinforcement Learning. The module serves as a taster for students considering further specialisation in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning . |
| **Content:**           | • Intro to AI: challenges, applications, ethics, explainability, transparency, fairness<br>• Basics of Search and Decisions (Tree/Graph Search, Local Search, Adversarial Search)<br>• Intro to Machine Learning and Deep Learning (Supervised and Unsupervised Learning, Learning Concepts from Examples, Classification and Regression Trees, Intro to Bayesian Networks, Intro to Neural Networks, Clustering.<br>• Basics of Reinforcement Learning (Agents, Policies, Optimisation, Value/policy Iteration, Temporal Difference Learning, SARSA, Q-learning)<br>• RL with Human Feedback, Transformers and intro to LLMs. |
